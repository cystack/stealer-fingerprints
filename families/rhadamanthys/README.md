<p>Rhadamanthys v0.9.x exports <code>userinformation.txt</code> as a flat hardware and locale block. <code>Wallpaper Hash</code>, <code>Traffic</code>, <code>Domain Name</code>, and <code>Display Resolution</code> form a distinctive field combination.</p>

## How to recognize it

- No stable text banner is known; combine filename and field layout.
- Recurring fields: <code>computer name</code>, <code>country</code>, <code>display resolution</code>, <code>domain name</code>, <code>hwid</code>, <code>installed ram</code>, <code>ip</code>, <code>keyboard language</code>, <code>log date</code>, <code>operation system</code>, <code>processor</code>, <code>system language</code>, <code>timezone</code>, <code>traffic</code>, <code>user language</code>, <code>user name</code>, <code>video card</code>, <code>wallpaper hash</code>
