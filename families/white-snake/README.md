<p>WhiteSnake exports <code>pc_info.txt</code> with system, user, network, and time fields. <code>Started as admin</code>, <code>Internet provider</code>, and <code>Installed antivirus</code> help distinguish the layout.</p>

## How to recognize it

- No stable text banner is known; combine filename and field layout.
- Recurring fields: <code>city</code>, <code>country</code>, <code>cpu</code>, <code>gpu</code>, <code>hwid</code>, <code>installed antivirus</code>, <code>internet provider</code>, <code>ip</code>, <code>ram</code>, <code>region</code>, <code>started as admin</code>, <code>system</code>, <code>system time</code>, <code>user name</code>
