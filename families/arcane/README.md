# Arcane

**Known malware family · high attribution confidence**

## At a glance

- Aliases: <code>Arcane Stealer</code>
- Typical filenames: <code>information.txt</code>
- Published formats: 1
- Representative samples: 1

<p>Arcane logs are recognized by <code>information.txt</code> and the combined <code>Worker</code>, <code>Date Install</code>, <code>External IP</code>, <code>Internal IP</code>, and <code>Gateway IP</code> fields. The observed layout continues with <code>=== ... Information ===</code> hardware and system sections. Public reporting describes VGS Stealer as a predecessor, not an Arcane alias.</p>

## How to recognize it

- No stable text banner is known; combine filename and field layout.
- Recurring fields: <code>capacity</code>, <code>caption</code>, <code>clipboard</code>, <code>date install</code>, <code>default</code>, <code>external ip</code>, <code>fullname</code>, <code>gateway ip</code>, <code>internal ip</code>, <code>manufacturer</code>, <code>model</code>, <code>name</code>, <code>product</code>, <code>size</code>, <code>speed</code>, <code>worker</code>
